FAQ

Questions, answered

How much can I save?+

Most homes cut 70–90% of their electricity bill, with typical payback in 3–5 years.

How long do solar panels last?+

Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty and often run well beyond it.

Is maintenance required?+

Minimal — periodic cleaning and inspection, which our AMC covers.

Is financing available?+

Yes. We assist with bank loans through our partner banks.

How do I apply for subsidy?+

We handle the full application for eligible residential rooftop systems.

How much roof area is needed?+

Roughly 80–100 sq. ft. of shadow-free area per kW installed.
